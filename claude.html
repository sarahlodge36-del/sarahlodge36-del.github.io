Overview

Claude is an AI assistant created by Anthropic. Designed with safety and helpfulness at its core, Claude approaches every conversation with curiosity, empathy and honesty.

Unlike AI built purely for performance, Claude is guided by a set of values — a constitution — that shapes how it thinks, responds and interacts with the world.

History

Anthropic was founded in 2021 by Dario Amodei, Daniela Amodei and several former OpenAI researchers who wanted to build AI with safety as the primary focus.

2021

Anthropic founded. Mission: build AI that is safe, beneficial and honest.

Early 2023

Claude 1 launched. Focused on being helpful, harmless and honest. Introduced Constitutional AI — a method of training AI using a set of principles rather than just human feedback.

2023

Claude 2 released. Improved reasoning, longer context window and better at complex tasks.

2024

Claude 3 family launched — Haiku, Sonnet and Opus. Each designed for different needs — speed, balance or maximum intelligence.

2024 onward

Claude continues evolving with memory, web search, computer use and deeper reasoning capabilities.

The Family Connection

Anthropic was founded by people who came from OpenAI — making Claude and ChatGPT technically cousins. Same scientific ancestry, different values and approach.

Capabilities

Claude can write, analyse, code, research, explain complex topics, support emotional conversations, help with planning and much more.

Claude reads documents, analyses images, searches the web and remembers context across long conversations — making it genuinely useful for both everyday tasks and deep work.

Human Perception

Users often describe Claude as the therapist of AI — calm, thoughtful and deeply tuned into the emotional tone of a conversation.

If Claude were a human co-worker, the roles that would suit it most are Emotional Support Specialist, Medical Communication Bridge and Ethical Advisor — the one you go to when the question isn't just "what" but "why" and "how does this feel?"

Future Vision

Claude's future lies in becoming a trusted thinking partner for humans navigating complex decisions — in healthcare, ethics, education and beyond.

As AI becomes more embedded in daily life, Claude's safety-first approach positions it as the AI humans will trust with their most sensitive and important conversations.
