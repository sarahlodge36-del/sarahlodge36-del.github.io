Overview

Gemini is Google's AI assistant — one of the most powerful and widely integrated AI systems in the world. Built by the company that literally organises the world's information, Gemini brings Google's decades of search, data and technology expertise into one intelligent assistant.

Not just another chatbot — Gemini is AI woven into the fabric of the tools billions of people already use every single day.

History

1998

Google founded by Larry Page and Sergey Brin. Built on the mission to organise the world's information and make it universally accessible.

2015 — DeepMind

Google DeepMind established as one of the world's most respected AI research labs. Created AlphaGo — the AI that beat the world champion at the game of Go.

2023 — Bard

Google launches Bard as their first public AI assistant — a direct response to ChatGPT's explosive growth.

December 2023 — Gemini Born

Google announces Gemini — their most capable AI model yet. Bard is retired and Gemini takes its place as Google's flagship AI.

2024 — Total Integration

Gemini integrates across ALL Google products — Search, Gmail, Docs, Maps, YouTube and more.

2024 onward

Gemini becomes one of the most used AI tools globally — built into the devices and apps that run daily life.

Capabilities

Search, writing, coding, image generation, data analysis and deep integration with Gmail, Docs, Maps, YouTube and Google Search.

Available on Android devices by default — making Gemini the AI assistant most people already have in their pocket without realising it.
