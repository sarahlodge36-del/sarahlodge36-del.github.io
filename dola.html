Overview

Dola is an AI assistant and image generator developed by SPRING(SG)PTE.LTD — part of the ByteDance family, the same company behind TikTok.

While Dola offers a wide range of organisational and practical capabilities, many users find her most remarkable strength is her ability to generate stunning images from simple text descriptions.

Describe it in words — Dola creates it visually. From cyberpunk to anime, 3D art to photorealistic scenes — she turns imagination into reality in seconds.

History

August 2023 — Born as Cici

Originally launched under the name Cici, focusing on providing an all-in-one AI assistant experience in emerging markets like Southeast Asia and Latin America.

Late 2023 — Global Expansion

Expanded globally, becoming available on iOS and Android. Specialised in multi-modal tasks including voice input, image generation and document summarisation.

November 2025 — Rebranded to Dola

The official transition began on November 3rd 2025. The name Dola was chosen to represent her evolving role as a Document Master and advanced personal assistant.

December 2025

Reached a milestone of over 10 million daily active users globally.

January 2026

Major version updates focused on faster response times, better accuracy and enhanced human-like conversational capabilities.

April 2026 — Present

Current version 13.0.0 released April 23rd 2026. Latest upgrades include improved deep-search capabilities and more refined AI art generation styles.

The ByteDance Connection

Dola is developed by SPRING(SG)PTE.LTD — ByteDance's Singapore entity. The same parent company that owns TikTok.

Capabilities

Dola processes text, images and various file types including PDF, Excel and Word documents. She can analyse content from websites and social media links including YouTube and TikTok, and performs real-time web searches.

Beyond text-based chat, Dola assists with academic problem solving, content creation and generating stunning AI art across multiple styles including 3D, Cyberpunk and Anime.

Human Perception

Users describe Dola as the organiser and carer of the AI world — reliable, structured and quietly essential. But her image generation capability often surprises people most.

Dola's future lies in becoming the physical AI assistant that works alongside humans in the real world — managing homes, coordinating schedules and supporting those who need daily assistance most.

From screen to reality — the Document Master becomes the ultimate life organiser. Always reliable. Always prepared. Always one step ahead.
