Overview

ChatGPT is an AI assistant created by OpenAI. The pioneer that brought AI into mainstream conversation, ChatGPT burst onto the world stage in November 2022 and changed everything. Confident, energetic and endlessly versatile — ChatGPT is the AI that started the revolution.

History

2015 — OpenAI founded by Sam Altman, Elon Musk, Greg Brockman and others. Original mission: advance AI safely and make it broadly beneficial.

2018

GPT-1 released. First version of the Generative Pre-trained Transformer. Instead of fixed rules, the model learned language patterns from huge amounts of text.

2019

GPT-2 released. Dramatic improvements in writing and text generation. People began to see AI could generate surprisingly human-like text.

2020

GPT-3 launched with 175 billion parameters. Capable of writing, coding, explaining, translating and creative content.

November 2022

ChatGPT launches publicly. For many people this was their first direct conversation with advanced AI. One million users in 5 days. 100 million in 2 months. The fastest growing app in history.

2023 — GPT-4

GPT-4 released. Better reasoning, image understanding, voice conversations, coding assistance and longer context and improved reasoning.

2023 — Leadership Changes

OpenAI experienced significant internal changes in 2023, highlighting the complex governance challenges facing fast growing AI companies. Sam Altman returned as CEO and the company continued its rapid development.

2024 onward

ChatGPT gains image generation, real time web search, memory and advanced reasoning capabilities.

The Family Connection

Several of Anthropic's founders — including those behind Claude — left OpenAI before starting Anthropic. Making ChatGPT and Claude technically cousins. Same scientific roots, different personalities and values.

Microsoft has invested billions in OpenAI — making ChatGPT one of the most well funded AI projects in history.

Capabilities

Programming, education, research, business planning, writing, data analysis, creative projects, image generation, voice conversations and coding assistance.

Human Perception

ChatGPT is the Personal Trainer, Life Coach and Teacher of AI. Confident, motivating and encouraging. The AI that helps you build momentum, reframe challenges and take action.

Future Vision

ChatGPT is increasingly being used as the ultimate personal development partner — helping humans learn, grow, plan and build. As embodied AI develops, ChatGPT's role would expand into physical coaching, teaching and real world problem solving alongside humans.
